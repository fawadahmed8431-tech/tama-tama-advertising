PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: September 2026 • Last Updated: September 2026
At Tam Tam Advertising, your trust and privacy are paramount to us. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you visit our website (tamtamadvertising.in), interact with our contact forms, or engage our advertising and digital marketing services.

01 Information We Collect

We only collect information that is necessary to deliver our services, communicate effectively with you, and improve your overall experience. The types of data we may collect include:

  • Personal & Contact Details: Full name, email address, telephone/mobile number, company or brand name, and any specific requirements or messages you voluntarily submit through our enquiry or contact forms.
  • Technical & Usage Data: IP address, device type, browser specifications, operating system, pages visited, time spent on pages, and referring links automatically gathered via standard web server logs and analytical cookies.
  • Communication Records: Enquiries, correspondence, notes, and records of discussions conducted via email, phone, or messaging platforms such as WhatsApp.

02 How We Use Your Information

We process your personal information strictly for legitimate commercial and operational purposes, including:

  • Responding to your project briefs, questions, and service enquiries.
  • Developing tailored advertising, branding, performance marketing, and web design proposals.
  • Managing our client relationship, billing, contracts, and service execution.
  • Improving our website performance, user interface, and digital offerings.
  • Complying with statutory, regulatory, and legal requirements under Indian law.

03 Legal Basis for Processing

We collect and handle your personal information in accordance with applicable data protection principles, relying upon:

  • Your Consent: Provided explicitly when submitting forms or reaching out to us.
  • Contractual Necessity: To take preparatory steps before entering into an agreement or executing contracted marketing campaigns.
  • Legitimate Interests: To improve our agency operations, protect against misuse, and maintain professional client communications.

04 Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information to third parties under any circumstances.

Your information is only shared under strict conditions:

  • Trusted Service Providers: Vetted third-party vendors assisting in our operations (such as secure email delivery infrastructure, cloud hosting, and analytics providers) who are legally obligated to maintain data confidentiality.
  • Legal & Regulatory Compliance: When required by court order, law enforcement, or statutory authorities to comply with applicable laws and protect our rights.

05 Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website utilizes cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance navigation and understand traffic patterns:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the core operation of our website and modal forms.
  • Analytical / Performance Cookies: Help us measure visitor counts, bounce rates, and popular content (e.g., Google Analytics) to refine our marketing materials.

You can manage or disable cookies at any time through your browser settings. Please note that disabling cookies may affect certain interactive features of the website.

06 Data Security & Retention

We employ appropriate technical, physical, and organizational security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure. Communications through our website are secured using standard SSL/TLS encryption.

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was gathered, maintain our commercial relationship, or satisfy legal and accounting requirements.

07 Your Rights & Choices

You have full control over your personal data. At any time, you may:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request corrections to any inaccurate or incomplete details.
  • Request the deletion or restriction of your personal data, subject to legal retention obligations.
  • Opt out of marketing communications by contacting us directly.

08 External Links & Third-Party Services

Our website features links to third-party platforms, including WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, and Google Maps. Once you leave our domain, our Privacy Policy no longer applies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites you visit.

09 Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, technological, or legal developments. Any revisions will be published immediately on this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
